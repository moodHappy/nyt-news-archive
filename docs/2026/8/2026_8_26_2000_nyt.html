China Artist Gao Zhen Jailed for 3 Years Over Satirical Mao Sculpture
艺术家高兟因创作讽刺毛泽东雕塑获刑三年

📅 2026-08-26 19:57 🔗 阅读原文 🔙 返回日历 📡 同步中...

A prominent artist who had relocated to New York was sentenced by a Chinese court on Tuesday to three years in prison for creating sculptures more than 15 years ago that allegedly defamed Mao Zedong, according to his wife.

据高兟妻子透露，中国一家法院周二判处这位已移居纽约的著名艺术家三年有期徒刑，理由是他曾在逾15年前创作了涉嫌亵渎毛泽东的雕塑。

The People's Court of Sanhe City in Hebei Province convicted Gao Zhen of infringing on the reputation and honor of heroes and martyrs, underscoring the increasingly shrinking space for expression under Chinese leader Xi Jinping.

河北省三河市人民法院裁定高兟侵害英雄烈士名誉、荣誉罪成立，这凸显了在中国领导人习近平治下日益缩窄的表达空间。

Gao, who had immigrated to the United States, was detained in Beijing two years ago while visiting family, in a case that highlights the opacity of the Chinese legal system. In March, the 70-year-old artist underwent a one-day secret trial from which his family and American diplomats were barred. During Tuesday's hearing, where he was found guilty and sentenced, two of Gao's brothers were permitted to attend.

已移居美国的高兟两年前回国探亲时在北京被拘留，他的案件凸显了中国法律体系的不透明性。今年3月，这位70岁的艺术家接受了为期一天的秘密聆讯，其家属和美国外交官被禁止旁听。在周二的聆讯中，他被判有罪并获刑，高的另外两个兄弟获准旁听。

Gao Zhen is the older brother in the art duo "Gao Brothers." The duo rose to prominence in the early 2000s for their satirical and provocative artistic expressions on politically sensitive topics, such as the Cultural Revolution—a decade of political turmoil launched by Mao—and the 1989 Tiananmen Square massacre.

高兟是艺术组合“高氏兄弟”中的哥哥。该组合在21世纪初因对政治敏感话题（如毛泽东发动的为期十年的政治动乱文化大革命以及1989年天安门广场大屠杀）进行具有讽刺意味和挑衅性的艺术表达而声名鹊起。

The three-year sentence is the maximum penalty Gao could have faced, meaning that after spending two years in detention, he will have to serve one more year in prison. The artist plans to appeal the court's decision.

三年刑期是高兟可能面临的最高量刑，这意味着在被羁押两年后，他还需在监狱服刑一年。这位艺术家计划对法院的判决提出上诉。

Gao Zhen's younger brother, Gao Qiang, who lives in New York and co-created the Mao sculptures with him, posted on Facebook before the sentencing that regardless of the court's verdict, "history will eventually judge this larger question: whether an artist will go to jail for confronting history through art."

高兟的弟弟高强现居纽约，曾与他共同创作这些毛泽东雕塑。在宣判前，高强在Facebook上发帖称，无论法院作何判决，“历史最终将对这个更大的问题作出评判：一个艺术家是否会因为通过艺术直面历史而入狱。”

"Gao Zhen has already lost his freedom for nearly two years. He should come home," Gao Qiang added.

“高兟已经失去了近两年的自由。他应该回家了，”高强补充道。

In 2024, Gao Zhen was arrested at his art studio in the suburbs of Beijing while visiting family in China. His wife, Zhao Yaliang, tried to return to the United States with their seven-year-old son, an American citizen, but was barred from leaving the country. The two have remained in China, waiting for Gao's release. According to Ms. Zhao, Gao has consistently refused to plead guilty.

2024年，高兟在回中国探亲期间于其位于北京郊区的艺术工作室被捕。他的妻子赵雅良试图带着他们身为美国公民的七岁儿子返回美国，但被限制出境。两人一直留在中国，等待高兟获释。据赵女士透露，高兟一直拒绝认罪。

The Gao Brothers made their name during a relatively open period in China. Although police had shut down their exhibitions in the past, the two were able to continue creating and became known for their inventive eccentricities while evading authorities. They held underground art exhibitions, the most notable of which featured a highly provocative sculpture—a bronze statue of a kneeling, repentant Mao Zedong titled Kneeling and Repentant Mao—they kept the head of the sculpture stored separately, only bringing it out for special viewings.

高氏兄弟是在中国相对开放的时期成名的。尽管警方曾关闭过他们的展览，但两人仍能继续创作，并因在躲避当局的同时进行富有创意的古怪行为而闻名。他们曾举办地下艺术展，其中最引人注目的是一件极具挑衅性的雕塑——一座名为《下跪忏悔的毛》的毛泽东下跪忏悔铜像——他们将雕塑的头部单独存放，只有在特别展出时才会拿出来。

According to Ms. Zhao, this piece was one of three works cited in the prosecution's evidence against Gao Zhen. The other two included a sculpture of Mao with breasts and a long Pinocchio nose, and another work depicting a group of Mao lookalikes raising their guns to execute Jesus.

据赵女士透露，这件作品是检方指控高兟的证据中涉及的三件作品之一。另外两件包括一个长着乳房和匹诺曹长鼻子的毛泽东雕塑，以及另一件描绘一群长相酷似毛泽东的人举枪准备处决耶稣的作品。

Criticism of former Chinese leader Mao Zedong is strictly controlled, and his political legacy is viewed as central to the Chinese Communist Party's legitimacy. As part of a campaign to enforce an official version of history, China has made it illegal to smear the reputation of historical figures or question official narratives.

对前中国领导人毛泽东的批评受到严格控制，其政治遗产被视为中国共产党合法性的核心。作为推行官方版本历史运动的一部分，中国已将玷污历史人物名誉或质疑官方叙事的行为定为非法。

Gao Zhen was prosecuted under a relevant defamation law passed in 2018, which was years after he had created these Mao sculptures. Journalists, stand-up comedians, and ordinary citizens have all been punished under the same law, but Gao's case is one of the first times the law has been applied retroactively.

高兟是被依据一项在2018年通过的相关诽谤法律起诉的，而这已经是他创作这些毛泽东雕塑多年之后的事情了。记者、脱口秀演员和普通公民都曾被依据同一法律受到过惩罚，但高的案件是该法首次被追溯适用的案例之一。

People familiar with the case said Gao Zhen was accused of spreading images of these Mao sculptures online as recently as 2024, including using them as the cover for a biography of Mao written by a Chinese journalist who emigrated to the U.S. in the 1980s.

熟悉该案的人士表示，高兟被控直到2024年还在网上散布这些毛泽东雕塑的图片，包括将其作为一位1980年代移居美国的中国记者所著的毛泽东传记的封面。

Shane Ye, a researcher at the nonprofit organization Chinese Human Rights Defenders, said that by targeting Gao Zhen, Chinese authorities were seizing the opportunity to send a warning to Chinese citizens abroad: whether they are overseas or return to China, the state still has jurisdiction over them.

非营利组织“中国人权捍卫者”的研究员谢恩·易（音）表示，通过将矛头对准高兟，中国当局借机向海外中国公民发出警告：无论他们身处国外还是回到中国，国家对他们依然拥有管辖权。

"But no matter which motive dominates: a man has been imprisoned for two years for his artistic creations, and his wife and young son, who is an American citizen, have been barred from leaving China during this time," he said.

他说：“但无论哪种动机占主导：一个人因为他的艺术创作而被关押了两年，而且他的妻子和身为美国公民的幼子在此期间一直被禁止离开中国。”

Ms. Zhao said her husband had been punished in prison, including being deprived of food and banned from reading books or receiving letters from his family.

赵女士说，她的丈夫在狱中受到了惩罚，包括被剥夺食物，被禁止阅读书籍以及接收家人的来信。

Despite this, Gao Zhen has continued to create art, tearing blank pieces of paper to make portraits of his family. In a recent letter to his wife, he included a poem: "Tossing and turning, sleepless in the night, past events are like clouds and smoke. Taming dragons in the past, now entering prison; serving time with no regrets, mocking the fierce and stubborn."

尽管如此，高兟仍在继续创作艺术，撕扯白纸制作家人的肖像。在最近给妻子的一封信中，他附上了一首诗：“翻来覆去夜无眠，往事如云亦如烟。昔日降龙今入狱，坐牢无悔戏凶顽。”
